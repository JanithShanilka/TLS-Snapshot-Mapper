Its static analysis, fingerprints, library-specific instrumentation, and client-random association must remain attributed to those authors.

The original supplied README remains in the separately retained retired-work archive; it is not copied into this clean saved-memory repository. The exact upstream base commit was not established by the earlier local import; retiring the experiment does not resolve that provenance gap. The public upstream project is [TLSKeyHunter](https://github.com/monkeywave/TLSKeyHunter).

The complete prior provenance record, notices, source hashes and unchanged retired sources are preserved in the local archive branch `codex/archive-live-hook-20260928` and the workspace archive `archive/retired-live-hook-20260928/`. They are historical records only. Their former requirements and contribution statements no longer govern active thesis work.

Active scope and contributions are defined in [THESIS_SCOPE.md](THESIS_SCOPE.md) and [CONTRIBUTION_BOUNDARIES.md](CONTRIBUTION_BOUNDARIES.md). Retiring the live-hook experiment does not transfer ownership of upstream ideas or establish novelty for the offline workflow.
